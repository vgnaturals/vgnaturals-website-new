**VG NATURALS PRIVATE LIMITED** is a Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh-based botanical ingredient manufacturer and supplier serving business enquiries across India. Our core botanical product ranges include Lemongrass (*Cymbopogon flexuosus*), Tulsi (*Ocimum sanctum*) and Sweet Basil (*Ocimum basilicum*), with essential oils and hydrosols presented as distinct ingredient formats.
